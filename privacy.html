Privacy policy

Your progress. Your trail.

How Suntrail Rangers stores your progress, handles support messages, and uses this website.

Effective 20 September 2026

Suntrail Rangers is developed by @samweel_sedrak. This policy covers the current game, our support correspondence, and this support website. For a privacy question or request, email samweel.sedrak@gmail.com.

Your game progress stays in local storage

Suntrail Rangers saves these records on your device:

  • Your sound, graphics, aim-assist, haptic-feedback and custom touch-layout settings, selected difficulty and chosen ranger.
  • Completed missions, which determine unlocked missions and weapons.
  • Mission best scores, overall best score and wave records, mission-star ratings for each difficulty, trophies, and lifetime eliminations and dashes.
  • Workshop parts earned in the game, crafted weapon modifications and the modification equipped for each weapon.
  • Collected ranger stories, discovered supply caches and optional scout rescues.
  • Selected ranger outfits, weapon colours and camp decorations.

Parts are earned through gameplay, without real-money purchases. Mission stars unlock cosmetic rewards using the best star count for each mission across difficulties; stars are not spent or removed when a reward is equipped.

The game does not save an unfinished mission for restoration after the app closes. Current health, ammunition, wave upgrades, and active enemies belong to the running game session.

The game has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking features. It does not send gameplay records to a Suntrail Rangers server and has no game-operated cloud-save service.

The game uses screen dimensions to fit its display and touch controls. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Our intended audience includes children, teenagers and adults. Gameplay does not ask for a player's name, email address or age, and there is no player messaging. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support on their behalf. Parents or guardians can use the support address for privacy questions or to request deletion of correspondence involving a child.

You choose what to share with support

If you email samweel.sedrak@gmail.com, the support mailbox receives your sending address, message, and any attachments you include. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue you describe.

The game does not send email or attach your progress or device information automatically. Email is sent and stored through your email provider and the recipient's email provider, whose privacy practices and retention rules also apply.

Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox. You can email the same address to request deletion of correspondence you have sent. Email providers manage their own backup and retention systems. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or unrelated sensitive information.

TestFlight testing is a separate Apple service

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. This can include device and operating-system details, installs, sessions, and invitation information. Feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight, including comments and screenshots, is also shared and may be associated with your name and email address.

We use beta reports to understand problems and improve Suntrail Rangers. TestFlight reports are stored and made available through Apple's service under its retention practices. Feedback you separately send by email is handled as described above. For a privacy request about information received by us, contact the support address; Apple manages its own service records. See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy information for the data involved and Apple's retention practices.

Google Play testing is a separate Google service

If you join an early-access or beta test through Google Play, Google collects and shares certain device and app-usage information and user-triggered events with the developer. Feedback you submit through Play is also available to the developer. We use those reports to improve the game; Google manages its service records under its own privacy practices. The game itself does not automatically send support messages or upload saved progress. See Google's testing information.

Your choices and device backups

You can change sound, graphics, aim assistance and touch controls in Settings, and choose difficulty and a ranger on the title screen. Haptic feedback is available on supported iPhones; the current Android version does not provide vibration. Expedition Camp lets you choose owned weapon modifications and unlocked cosmetic rewards. Starting a new mission replaces the current run; it does not erase earned progress, discoveries or your best records.

Deleting the installed app removes its local game data. Clearing the app's storage on Android also removes local records. Offloading an app on iPhone can retain its data. Copies may remain in device or cloud backups, and restoring a backup may restore game progress.

Your operating system and backup provider determine whether app data is backed up, restored, or retained. Manage those copies through your device and backup settings. Suntrail Rangers does not control those systems or provide a separate account-based backup. Apple explains app-data backups in What does iCloud back up?

On Android, app data may be included in device backups and restored depending on your Android version, device, app support and backup settings. Google or your device provider manages those copies. See Google's Android backup guide.

A simple support website

This website contains no tracking scripts, analytics tools, advertising pixels, contact forms, remote fonts, or embedded third-party content. Its pages and styles do not set cookies or use browser storage. Images and styles are served with the site.

When this website is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub records and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, including when a visitor is not signed into GitHub. This hosting information is separate from locally saved game progress. Read GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for the host's practices.

Email links open your email app. When you choose a link to another website, that site's privacy practices apply.

Questions and policy changes

This policy may be updated when the game, support arrangements, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.

For questions or a privacy request, contact samweel.sedrak@gmail.com.
