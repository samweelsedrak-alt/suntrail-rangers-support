Choose your challenge

Easy, Medium, or Hard. Complete each mission's objectives and five waves, with upgrades between waves and a final boss.

Build your loadout

Unlock the shotgun and marksman rifle. Earn parts from machines and discoveries, then craft weapon modifications with benefits and tradeoffs at camp.

Make camp your own

Mission stars unlock ranger outfits, weapon colours and camp decorations. Stars are never spent: your best rating per mission counts toward rewards.

Suntrail Rangers is available to invited testers through TestFlight. The first public release is planned for iPhone. The game is not yet available to download from the public App Store. Release information will appear here when it is ready.

Play locally, with no game account, ads, or in-app purchases. Once installed, gameplay works offline. Progress, mission stars, workshop parts and modifications, discoveries, cosmetics and settings stay on your device. Customize the touch controls for your preferred layout.
