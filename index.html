Newer beta: a longer trail, a friend alongside

The newer iPhone beta keeps all 24 solo missions, ranger skills, operations and camp progress; these features are also planned for the Android 1.1.0 update. Optional Duo Survival brings two players to the Glasshouse arena for five shared waves. One player hosts a private room and shares its code with a friend. Both need a compatible Duo-enabled version and an internet connection; this feature is not available in earlier six-mission builds.

Duo uses an anonymous Unity guest account and encrypted Unity Relay connections. There are no player names, chat, voice or public matchmaking. Solo progress stays local. Read about online service information and guest deletion in the Duo privacy section. This page does not announce App Store availability or guarantee a TestFlight invitation.

In the upcoming Android update, Duo online is available to players aged 16 or older after a neutral age-range choice. Every solo mode remains available to all players. Only the eligibility choice is saved on the device; no birthday or precise age is requested. The iPhone build 15 beta is unchanged by this Android restriction.
