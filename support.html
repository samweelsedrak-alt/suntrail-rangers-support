Getting started

How do I move, aim, and fire?

Hold your iPhone sideways. By default, drag the left stick to move and the right stick to aim and fire. Lift your aiming thumb to stop firing. Tap Dash, Reload, your ranger ability or an unlocked weapon slot. In Settings > Touch controls, move the action buttons, resize the sticks or swap sides for left-handed play.

Which ranger can I choose?

Choose Finn, Maya or Rook from the title screen. Finn has Trail Dash, Maya has Field Heal, and Rook has Aegis Shield. Each ranger's ability has a cooldown. Outfits and weapon colours change appearance without adding stat bonuses.

How do I unlock another mission or weapon?

Complete the mission objectives and all five waves in Sundune Depot to unlock Verdant Station and the Breach Shotgun. Complete Verdant Station to unlock Frostline Harbor and the Longshot DMR. Continue through Sunspire Mesa, Moonlit Marsh and Ember Foundry. Use the journey arrows on the title screen to choose among all six arenas as you unlock them. Completing a single wave does not unlock the next mission.

Can I change the difficulty?

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard from the title screen before starting a mission. Easy gives more room to learn the controls and enemy patterns. Your earned mission and weapon unlocks remain available across difficulties.

What happens between waves?

After each of the first four waves, choose one upgrade: Overclock for stronger shots, Reinforce for more health, or Mobility for faster movement and a shorter dash cooldown. These upgrades apply to your current run. Each mission has five waves and a final boss.

How does the Workshop work?

Open Expedition Camp > The Workshop before a mission. Defeat machines and find arena discoveries to earn parts in the game; parts cannot be bought with real money. Craft a modification once, then switch freely among owned choices at camp. Equip one modification per weapon. Each has a benefit and a tradeoff, and a weapon must be unlocked before you can craft for it.

How do I earn star rewards?

Each mission shows three star criteria before you start: complete the objectives, meet its protection goal and finish within its active-time target. Your best winning-run rating is saved separately for each difficulty. Star Rewards counts your highest star count per mission across difficulties, up to 18. These stars unlock outfits for all three rangers, weapon colours and camp decorations. Stars are never spent. You can preview a locked reward, but must earn its milestone before equipping it.

What can I discover in the arenas?

Approach a service bay to open its gate, then look for a parts cache and a scout awaiting pickup. A separate survey beacon holds a ranger story. Move close and tap the displayed action to collect a discovery, then read your notes in Expedition Camp > Field Journal. Discoveries stay recorded and award parts once. Watch the marked press area before its machinery activates; it can damage you and enemies. Conveyor belts move actors through the arena. These exploration activities are optional.

Can I pause or switch to another app?

Tap Pause to take a break, then Resume when you are ready. Leaving active gameplay for another app should pause the mission until you resume. If the operating system closes the game in the background, the next launch returns to the title screen.

What progress is saved?

Completed missions and weapon unlocks, best records, mission-star ratings, trophies, lifetime eliminations and dashes, workshop parts and modifications, discovered stories/caches/scouts, cosmetic choices, selected ranger and settings are saved on your device. Custom touch layouts are saved too. An unfinished mission's health, ammunition and temporary wave upgrades are not restored after closing the app. There is no game account or game-operated cloud-sync feature. Deleting the app or moving to another device may lose local progress; operating-system backups are managed separately.

Does the game need an internet connection?

After installation, gameplay works offline. Downloading or updating through Apple, using TestFlight, visiting this site, and sending support email may need a connection.

What should I do if something looks wrong?

Pause and note the mission, difficulty, wave, and device orientation. Tell support which part of the screen is affected, especially if a button, label, or character is clipped. Send the steps that reproduce the issue and an original screenshot if possible. Avoid deleting and reinstalling the app before contacting support if you want to preserve local progress.

Where can I download Suntrail Rangers?

The game is available to invited testers through TestFlight and is not yet on the public App Store. If you already have a test invitation, use the invitation and Apple's TestFlight app. Public release information will appear on the game page.

Sending email shares your address, message, and attachments with the support mailbox. The privacy policy explains support messages, local saves, TestFlight, and website hosting.
